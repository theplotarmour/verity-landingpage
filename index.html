It knows.

Ask in plain words. Answers come back from your own entries — with the note, the date and the record they came from attached.

If the record doesn't say it, Verity doesn't either.

Every answer names the entries it came from. Nothing is inferred from outside your own data, and nothing is trained on it.

Who runs on it

Four trades. One spine each.

The record changes shape by trade. What never changes is that there is only one of it, and everyone downstream reads the same one.

Fabrication & machine shops

Quote by weight and process. Cutting, welding, QC as stages on the order — not a WhatsApp thread. Sheet stock draws down as the job moves.

Contractors & site services

A site becomes a project

Milestones, not month-ends. Material issued to a site stays attached to that site, and the running bill is the project — not a separate sheet someone rebuilds each Friday.

Distribution & trading

A godown becomes a number you trust

Multi-location stock, batch and expiry, dispatch against the order that reserved it. Reorder points fire off real movement instead of a memory of last month.

  • ReordersOn movement, not hunch

Assembly & light manufacturing

A BOM becomes a shortage warning

The bill of materials knows what a build consumes, so a confirmed order tells you what is short before the line stops — not on the morning it stops.

  • WarnsShort before start
  • TracksShift output per line
